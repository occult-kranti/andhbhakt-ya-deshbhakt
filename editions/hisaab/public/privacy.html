Your play. Your choices.

Updated 27 September 2026.

Local progress

Your solo profile, answers, progress, preferences and device circles live in this browser's storage. No email account is required to play. The site host receives the ordinary network information needed to serve a webpage, including an IP address and browser request details. Fonts are bundled with the site.

Online beta

Connecting to Online beta creates a guest profile on our Supabase game server in India. The server stores a chosen nickname, a random player identifier, match participation, submitted answers, server timestamps, scores, competition records and online circle memberships. Other players see your nickname and the results of shared games; competition tables show qualifying players' nicknames and scores. Choose a nickname that does not identify you if you prefer.

This browser saves a secret guest credential so you can return. Treat it like a password: do not publish it or include it in bug reports. Clearing browser storage or moving to another device can lose access to that guest profile. The server stores a hash of the credential, not the credential itself. A credential expires after 30 days; expiry alone does not erase historical records. Online progress is separate from solo XP and is not imported from your browser's learning history.

The server uses a salted hash of a network address for abuse limits. Supabase and the website host process ordinary network and operational logs to provide the service. No advertising profile is built from your answers. Read Supabase's privacy information.

Online circles

Online circle names, invitations, memberships and personal group nicknames are stored by the game server. Members can see the group's roster and your chosen group nickname. Anyone holding an invite code can request to join; share it only with people you want in the group. Leave a group from its online controls. A nickname is a chosen label, not a verified identity. Do not put addresses, phone numbers or sensitive family information in names.

Device circles and direct friend connections

The original device circles are a separate casual feature. Their invite links and codes are shared by you. Anyone holding an invite can join; leaving removes this browser's copy but does not revoke an invite already shared. A device circle is not an automatically synced membership service.

Choosing Connect circle shares your group nickname and self-reported device XP with connected invite holders. This browser saves the circle, its secret invite, your group nickname and up to 32 previously met nicknames. Circle renaming changes this device and new invites; it does not update every existing member.

When you start a friend duel or connect a circle, public connection services help the browsers find each other. Their operators see connection metadata, and the other player may learn your internet address through WebRTC. In a duel, your chosen name, answers and answer times are sent to the other player. In a connected circle, nickname and XP updates go to connected participants. Solo play does not use this friend connection.

Advertising choices

Advertising is off in the default release. No AdSense script is loaded in that configuration. If advertising is enabled on this site, it is limited to a labeled placement after the home page’s main content. No ads appear in questions, live rounds, circles or before a result receipt. Ads never award XP or change an answer.

The advertising integration requires an adult audience decision and an affirmative consent signal from the site's configured consent management platform before requesting an ad. Unknown audience or missing consent means no ads. The integration requests non-personalized ads; that does not mean no storage or network processing. Google and its partners may process device and request information when you consent. Read how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

When advertising is enabled, use Ad privacy choices at the bottom of the home page to reopen the consent platform and withdraw consent. Withdrawal reloads the page to stop the advertising runtime. Declining ads does not stop play.

Manage or delete your data

Use Settings to export or delete local game progress. This does not delete online records. Use Online beta to delete your online guest profile while you still have its credential. This removes your circle memberships and public competition identity; an opponent's historical game outcome may remain without your nickname. Use circle controls to leave individual groups. Browser data deletion cannot recall copies you already shared with another person, and should follow online deletion if you want both removed.

Links, sharing and contact

Opening a source, WhatsApp, GitHub or a share destination takes you to another service governed by its own privacy terms. A correction posted to GitHub is public; do not include private information. For the available contact routes, see Contact & corrections.
