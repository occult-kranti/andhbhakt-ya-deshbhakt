Your profile. Your privacy.

Your required player profile

This beta is for adults aged 18 or older. Before playing, choose a public nickname and provide your email address. We store your email privately as unverified contact information, together with your profile preferences, an adult self-attestation and the accepted beta-terms version. We do not send a verification email or marketing email. An email address is not proof of identity, and entering the same email never opens or links another profile.

What is public and what stays private

Other players can see your nickname, shared match results and qualifying leaderboard entries. Your email, recovery code and browser credential are excluded from leaderboards, duel opponents, certificates and share links. Do not put an email address, phone number or sensitive personal details in your public nickname.

Access and recovery

Your browser saves a random access credential; the game server stores its hash. A browser session lasts 30 days. A separate recovery code lets you restore your server profile, coins and online progress on another browser or after the session expires. Save the code privately when it is shown. It grants access to the profile; do not post it in an issue or share it with another player.

Email alone cannot restore a profile because email ownership is not checked. Restoring with a valid recovery code replaces the old browser access credential. Replacing a recovery code invalidates the previous recovery code. Deleting a profile revokes both. If you lose both the browser credential and recovery code, this beta has no verified-email recovery service. Session expiry does not delete the profile or historical game records.

Game data and service providers

The game stores a random player identifier, profile fields, match participation, submitted answers, server timestamps, scores, coin transactions and competition results in the game's private Supabase database in India. The server uses a salted network-address hash for abuse limits. GitHub Pages serves the website; GitHub and Supabase process ordinary network and operational information required to provide their services. See GitHub's privacy statement and Supabase's privacy policy.

Server records are retained until you delete the profile, with limited historical match and transaction records retained without your profile email or public nickname where necessary to preserve your opponents' results and settlement records. Hosting logs and backups follow the provider's applicable retention settings; deletion is not a promise to erase every backup immediately. We do not sell profile information or infer advertising interests from your quiz answers.

Device progress, photos and sharing

Practice XP, receipt history, preferences and other device progress are stored in this browser. Restoring the online profile does not automatically transfer these local records. Optional profile photos stay on your device. A saved avatar choice may sync as a preset identifier; a photo file is not uploaded to Supabase. Sharing or downloading a result or certificate is your choice. Copies you send to another service cannot be recalled by deleting this profile.

Circles and shared-device play

Online circles store group names, invitations, memberships and group nicknames. Circle members see the group roster. Share invite codes only with intended participants. Device circles are separate local records; connected device circles may exchange nickname and self-reported device progress through peer connections. Such connections can expose connection metadata or your IP address to connection providers and the peer. Shared-device play uses the current device's local records.

Advertising

Ads are disabled in this published beta until the operator completes hosting, publisher and consent configuration. The integration records potential breaks after two duel wins or three duel losses, and after supported practice completions. A break is offered between games after the result; it never changes a score, grants wager credit or blocks zero-coin play. Provider availability, frequency limits and format mean a video is not guaranteed.

When properly configured, an adult-audience decision and affirmative consent from the configured consent platform are required before an ad request. Declining, missing consent, no fill or an ad error leaves play available. Non-personalized ads can still involve storage and network processing. See Google's partner-site information. The operator must make consent choices and withdrawal available before enabling ads.

Export and deletion

Use the profile controls to export your server profile or delete it while you hold a valid credential. Exported profile data includes your own email, so keep the download private. Deleting removes private profile fields, revokes access and removes the public competition identity. Leaving or deleting during a started wager follows the disclosed forfeit rules. Delete the online profile before clearing browser storage if you want both removed. Device-progress export/reset controls are separate.

Operator and contact

This beta is published by the maintainers of the occult-kranti/andhbhakt-ya-deshbhakt project. The repository handle is not a verified legal-entity name. A private operator/grievance mailbox has not yet been supplied; this remains a launch prerequisite. The contact page lists the current public correction route. Do not send private data through a public issue.
