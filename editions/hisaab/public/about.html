HISAAB DO · The public-money edition

Questions deserve receipts.

Janta ka paisa. Janta ka sawaal.

HISAAB DO is a browser quiz about Indian public life: government spending, schemes, institutions, elections, news ownership and the public record. Play today's five questions, follow a state or sector file, trace money through the years, or challenge a friend.

Read the source

Every answer carries a source. Questions about disputes distinguish allegations from findings and show the date of the recorded status. A source can be revised and a court case can change: use the dated receipt and original record together.

Satire with a clear target

The rank names poke fun at political labelling and blind devotion. They are not findings about a person's politics, character, religion, caste or community. HISAAB DO is an independent project, not a government service or a political party publication.

Play at your own pace

XP records progress in the game and has no cash value. Friends and families can use circles and choose a nickname for each group. Solo progress is stored on this device; friend matches use a direct browser connection and are casual, trust-based games.

Corrections belong in the record

Spot an error? Send the question ID, the claim and a source using the correction form. Our rules and corrections log explain the editorial approach.
