Janta ka paisa. Janta ka sawaal.

Andhbhakt ya Deshbhakt is a browser quiz about Indian public life: government spending, schemes, institutions, elections, news ownership and the public record. Open today's file, follow a state or sector file, trace money through the years, or challenge a friend.

Read the source

Every answer carries a source. Questions about disputes distinguish allegations from findings and show the date of the recorded status. A source can be revised and a court case can change: use the dated receipt and original record together.

Satire with a clear target

The rank names poke fun at political labelling and blind devotion. They are not findings about a person's politics, character, religion, caste or community. Andhbhakt ya Deshbhakt is an independent project, not a government service or a political party publication.

Play at your own pace

XP records progress in the game and has no cash value. Friends and families can use circles and choose a nickname for each group. Solo progress is stored on this device. Duels pair two people, with optional free game-coin stakes. A zero-stake duel stays available at any balance. Untimed practice files, direct friend play and pass-and-play offer other ways to learn.

A referee for online play

Online beta uses a game server to lock answers, keep time and settle matches. Choose public matchmaking, a private friend room or a tournament queue. Correctness comes first, then response time, with a small window for tied times. Network delay still matters. Guest identities and the public learning bank mean these are recreational competitions, not cheat-proof or cash-prize events. Online records are separate from device XP.

Corrections belong in the record

Spot an error? Send the question ID, the claim and a source using the correction form. Our rules and corrections log explain the editorial approach.
