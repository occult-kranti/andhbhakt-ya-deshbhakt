Beta terms

Version: beta-1. These are the current rules of participation in this adult testing beta. The operator must complete its legal identity, private support contact and jurisdiction review before a general public or monetized launch.

Who can play

You must be at least 18 and create a profile with a public nickname and an email address you are entitled to provide. The age declaration is self-attested; the beta does not verify identity or email ownership. Do not impersonate another person. Keep your browser access and recovery codes private. Read the privacy notice before creating the profile.

Free simulated coins

The games are free to play. Coins are in-game scores, with no cash value, purchase, deposit, redemption or prize. An optional stake uses only these free coins and defaults to zero. Zero balance never prevents a zero-stake duel. Coins, profiles and rewards must not be sold, transferred for money or used to promise anything of monetary value outside the game.

Human duels and settlement

Online competitive duels connect real players, with five rounds and a 30-second limit for each question. The server locks the first accepted answer and decides the result using correct-answer count and server-measured total response time. A difference within 120 milliseconds is a draw. Network delay can affect timing; equal geographic latency is not promised.

Both players confirm the same optional stake before it is reserved. The winner receives the combined stake. A draw, pre-start cancellation or applicable system cancellation refunds reserved stakes. After both players are ready, leaving or deleting a profile forfeits the stake to the opponent. One player absent for more than 90 seconds while the opponent remains active forfeits; when both are absent, reserved stakes are refunded. Completion rewards are separate from the pot and follow the visible qualification and daily limits.

Fair play and conduct

Do not automate play, collude to farm coins, exploit bugs, harass other players or use abusive or misleading nicknames. The beta may be paused to fix faults. Leaderboard labels and achievements describe game activity; they do not verify a person's knowledge, identity, political beliefs or official status.

Satire and sourced questions

The illustrated character is an original satirical, Modi-inspired caricature. The game, its certificates and progress labels are not endorsed by the person depicted, any political party or the Government of India. “UPI tax savings” is a humorous name for free simulated coins, not a UPI service, money, savings product or tax advice. Read each dated source receipt and report factual errors through the correction controls.

Ads and sharing

Ads are currently disabled. If configured later, potential video/interstitial breaks occur between games, subject to consent, availability and provider rules. Ads never decide an answer, score or stake. Sharing is voluntary; include only content or profile images you have permission to use.

Help and data controls

Use profile controls for recovery-code management, export or deletion, and Contact & corrections for the available reporting route. No verified-email recovery is available in this beta. These product rules do not claim regulatory approval or remove rights you have under applicable law.
