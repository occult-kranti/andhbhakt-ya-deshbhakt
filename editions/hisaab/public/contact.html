Put it on the record.

A question needs correcting

Open the correction form. Include the question ID printed on the receipt, what needs changing, and an original source. The form prepares a report; it does not silently submit one.

A bug, accessibility issue or suggestion

Use the project's GitHub issue tracker. Include the HISAAB DO page, what happened, and the browser/device if relevant. Review your report before posting.

The tracker is public. A private contact mailbox has not been supplied for this release. Do not publish personal, sensitive or confidential information in an issue. If GitHub asks you to sign in, you can still use the in-game correction form to prepare and copy a draft.

Read the policy

The rules page describes sourcing, dated legal statuses, rights of reply and the correction log. The privacy notice explains local data and friend connections.
