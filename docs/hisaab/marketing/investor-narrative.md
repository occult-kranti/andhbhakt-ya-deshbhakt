# Investor narrative

Version: 27 September 2026. This narrative describes an early playable product, not a traction-stage business. Server competition is a beta under construction until release evidence confirms publication.

## 1. HISAAB DO

**Janta ka paisa. Janta ka sawaal.**

A bilingual browser game about public money, with a source-linked receipt after each answer.

Presenter: demonstrate one question before giving a long explanation. Do not claim political neutrality as a certification. Explain the editorial rules: target the habit of labelling people, preserve legal status and counterpoints, and make corrections visible.

## 2. The product bet

Public-money questions can become a reason to play together. The working hypothesis is that a short, humorous game lowers the effort required to open a source, and that familiar friends give players a reason to return.

Initial recruitment cohorts are volunteers from quiz groups, campus societies, civic learning communities and existing family/friend circles. These are proposed pilot cohorts, not proven customers or measured segments. No TAM estimate appears because no defensible market research was supplied.

## 3. A playable foundation

The existing browser release supports English/Hindi, daily play, topical files, state and sector discovery, money trails, bots, friend play and pass-and-play. Source links, a receipt and personal progression connect the answers to public records. Correct base-answer XP is 30 below 8 seconds, 20 below 15 seconds and 10 at 15 seconds or more. Wrong answers earn zero base-answer XP. Other explicitly named progression rewards are separate.

The 389 figure in earlier project documents refers to exported money-ledger rows. It is not a count of questions or players and is omitted from the deck.

## 4. A duel with a referee

The online beta places the official answer lock, deadline and match result on a server. Correctness comes first; reaction time distinguishes correct answers subject to the implemented draw band. Immediate personal feedback follows the locked answer. The shared verdict waits for both valid answers or the deadline.

The intended design uses durable transactions and idempotent commands, not a physics simulation. Guest identities are device bound. Existing practice questions are public, so this is not a secret question bank or a cheat-proof sport. Network delay remains observable and the interface must explain degraded conditions. Never say the game guarantees equal reaction-time measurement.

## 5. Reasons to return

Circles provide a familiar place and a nickname chosen for that group. Daily and weekly competition windows create fresh starts. A receipt offers a next step even after a wrong answer. Earned honours should expire clearly while permanent personal progress remains intact.

These mechanisms are hypotheses to test. Avoid loss of earned XP for missed days, guilt prompts, invitation spam, endless queue pressure or cash prizes. Keep the hidden honour out of public launch copy, thumbnails and pre-earn ladders.

## 6. Distribution starts with one useful invitation

Use a source-linked question as the discovery unit. A creator or community host can share a clip of one round, then invite an existing group to a scheduled session. Measure a completed first round and a successful return, not follower counts alone. Recruit opt-in pilots before buying broad reach. Never send unsolicited invites or suggest that a real community endorses the product without consent.

## 7. Commercial experiments

Possible later options: clearly labelled sponsorship of editorial spaces, approved display ads outside active play, and optional cosmetic editions or hosted community events. These are hypotheses, not shipped payment features. Source selection and answer correctness remain outside commercial control. Keep play and receipts unobstructed. Do not imply AdSense approval or forecast a CPM without evidence.

## 8. The first evidence to collect

Activation: a new eligible identity completes a first round and opens its receipt.
Return: eligible cohorts return on day 1 and day 7, with confidence intervals and count denominators.
Social usefulness: invited users join and complete a duel, with opt-in attribution.
Fairness: match completion, error rate, latency distribution, duplicate-settlement failures, suspicious repeats and reports.

No baseline exists in this pack. Agree event definitions and collect a baseline before setting investment claims. Guest IDs approximate devices, not people. Minimise data and publish the privacy basis before analytics activation.

## 9. Launch stages

Playable now: existing browser game and source-linked solo/social features.
Online beta under construction: server duels, competition windows, title expiry and operational monitoring.
Gates before expansion: repeatable multi-device checks, editorial refresh, reporting route, budget controls, reliable recovery design and a small opt-in community pilot.

Do not advertise a public tournament schedule until its exact rules, UTC times, capacity, tie handling and outage policy are displayed and tested.

## 10. Investment milestones

The use-of-funds discussion begins with content stewardship, dependable multiplayer operations and measured distribution experiments. The founder must supply the raise amount, runway, terms, company details, team biographies, ownership and budget. Each proposed tranche should buy evidence: a stable pilot, a repeatable cohort result, then a supportable cost per completed match.

The live demo is available at https://hisaab-do.whatswrong-inc.chatgpt.site. Traction, revenue and financing terms are not supplied. This deck can support an introductory product discussion; it is not a complete investment memorandum.
